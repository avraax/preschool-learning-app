# De-emoji PRD-01 — replace or remove EVERY shipped emoji

**Date:** 2026-07-31
**Owner:** Allan. **Target user:** ~5-year-old Danish boy, iPad landscape, pre-reader.
**Art:** partly art-GATED — a small new baked batch (§6). Every workstream is independently shippable;
W1 (adult tools) and W2 (deletions) need **no art at all**.

> The app is baked soft-3D everywhere that matters — worlds, section icons, game subjects, symbols,
> mascots. The emoji that remain are the visible seams in that language: flat 2D, a different palette,
> and rendered by the **OS font**, so they change shape between the iPad (iPadOS 17.7 floor) and any
> newer device, and a few don't exist at all on 17. This PRD removes all of them: art where the glyph
> carries meaning, a real icon where it's a UI affordance, and deletion where it's filler.

## 10. Risks

- **Layout shift** is the main one: emoji are text (font-sized, inline) and art is a sized `<img>`.
  Anything that used `fontSize` to scale a glyph needs an explicit width/height. Measure, don't eyeball.
- **`AppErrorBoundary`** must not gain a fragile import (see §3).
- **The confetti set is 30 of the 118 distinct glyphs** — option (a) collapses it to zero art, but the
  particle count/perf profile must stay the same (`react-confetti` rides the lazy chunk; don't pull art
  into first paint).
- **iPadOS 17.7 floor**: verify on the real device, not just headless — the whole point is that OS-font
  glyphs differ there.
