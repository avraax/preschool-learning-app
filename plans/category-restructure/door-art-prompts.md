# Door art — Category restructure 01

One render: the **Bogstaver og Ord** door (PRD §2 D7). Farver og Krea reuses the palette; Tal and
Engelsk keep theirs.

**Attach as STYLE references:** `art-src/icons/alphabet.png` (it is also the CONTENT reference — the
book stack must look like this one) and `art-src/icons/colors.png`.

**Paste this (one image, so no batch-of-4 opener):**

> Generate 1 image: a single centered subject, soft-3D claymation style, Pixar-lite, rounded matte clay,
> soft top-left key light, gentle rim light, soft contact shadow, warm and child-safe, slight 3/4
> top-down angle, flat solid #00FF00 background edge to edge, square 1:1, highest resolution.
>
> Subject: the stack of four books from the attached reference (blue, yellow, coral, blue — same
> shapes, same pastel clay), with three chunky, thick, rounded clay capital letters standing in front of
> and leaning against the stack: **A** (red), **B** (yellow), **Æ** (blue — the Danish letter, A and E
> joined into one glyph). The letters are as tall as two books, very thick and puffy like toy
> fridge-magnet letters, each letter clearly correct and readable. No other text anywhere. Nothing
> green in the subject. Remember: flat solid #00FF00 behind it, no table, no scene.

**Check before handing back:** the three letters must read as A, B and Æ at a small size. If the Æ comes
out as Œ, AE side by side, or a garbled shape, retry once with "the Æ is ONE glyph: an A whose right leg
is the vertical stem of an E". If it fails again, stop — the PRD's fallback is the plain book stack.

**Save** via right-click → "Save image as…" (not the embedded download button — it stamps a ✦ and can
add stray elements). Name it `letters.png` and drop it in `art-src/icons/`.
